# Seller Center Stitch implementation

Reference: `stitch_helacart_seller_dashboard_ui.zip` (seven screens and `lankan_merchant_flow/DESIGN.md`). Seller Center stays in English whatever language the buyer storefront uses.

| Stitch screen                        | Next.js route                                   | Implementation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Seller overview                      | `/seller`                                       | Merchant sidebar and top bar, welcome card, store readiness, product/order/stock metrics, recent COD orders and inventory health.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Products list                        | `/seller/products`                              | Status tabs, category and stock filters, server-side search and pagination, CSV export, approval actions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Create/edit product                  | `/seller/products/new`, `/seller/products/[id]` | Core details, photo upload, review feedback, listing approval controls, variants (add, and edit SKU/options/price of non-default variants) and stock.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Product variants and stock           | `/seller/inventory`, product detail             | On hand minus reserved summary, stock matrix, adjustment form and stock history.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Seller orders                        | `/seller/orders`                                | Status filters (incl. delivery failed / returned), order timeline, itemized COD amount, address and contact. Actions: accept → pack → ship (courier details) → delivered (COD collected); **delivery failed** (reason), **re-attempt delivery** (new courier details), **returned to me** (reason); cancel requires a reason. Status reasons appear in the detail and history. |
| Delivery settings                    | `/seller/settings`                              | District rates, free-delivery threshold and delivery days. Owner only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Seller application and access states | `/seller/apply`, `/seller`                      | Application form, pending/rejected/verification states.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

Roles and suspension (API contract §5–§6): `/auth/me` memberships provide `role`, `sellerStatus` and `statusReason`. Managers can view but not save shop profile, business details and delivery settings, and cannot record refunds, submit commission payments or manage the team. A suspended shop shows a banner with the admin's reason; write-only workspaces are read-only, while orders, shipments and support replies keep working.

The source mockups also contain settlement balances, courier integrations and delivery guarantees that the API does not supply, so they are not shown as live data. Live Firebase, Neon, R2 and COD flows require your own configuration and real account testing.
